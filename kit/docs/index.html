Puppi3's Creator Kit

The kit's guides.

A Ghost site with its own analytics, a live stream, every post shared to the social networks, and every network's numbers in one place. These guides are for whoever sets one up and runs it, whoever posts on it, and whoever reads its numbers.

For installers

Setup Guide

From nothing to a running site: what you need, the site's folder, Docker Compose or Kubernetes (your own cluster, Amazon EKS, Google GKE), and the steps to finish by hand.
For whoever runs a site

Operations Guide

Keeping it running: updating the kit, backups and putting one back, moving between machines, Docker Compose, Kubernetes and the clouds, and troubleshooting.
For whoever runs a site

Networks Guide

Connecting each network, for sharing posts and for the numbers: the app to make on Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, SoundCloud and the rest, its keys, the traps on the way, and checking the numbers arrive.
For whoever runs a site

Cloud Guide

A busy stretch on Amazon EKS or Google GKE and back home: Cloudflare's cache first, what it costs, setting up each cloud once, the move, and tearing it all down.
For the site's owner

Theme Guide

What visitors see and what you change in Ghost Admin: the theme's settings, the LIVE NOW bar, the Social preview button, links to your other platforms, the short links, and using another theme.
For whoever posts

Posting Guide

Writing a post so it shares well: what goes to Bluesky, Threads, Instagram and TikTok, the tags, the phone notifications, and finishing Instagram and TikTok posts.
For the site's owner

Analytics Guide

Reading the dashboards: who visits and from where, what turns visitors into members, how each post did, the newsletter, live shows and when to post.
For installers

Settings Reference

Every setting a site has: site.env, each part's secrets, the Kubernetes and cloud files. What each one does, its default, and what setup fills in for you.
