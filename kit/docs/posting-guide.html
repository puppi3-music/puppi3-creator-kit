How it works

When you publish a post on example.com, it's shared out automatically. Bluesky and Threads get posted for you. Instagram and TikTok are left for you to finish, because those are the apps where you add music and tag the artists.

About this guide

It describes the kit's settings as they come. Whoever runs the site can change which networks get posts, what posts by itself and the tags' names (in site.env), so ask them if something here doesn't match. example.com stands for your site's address.

Ghostyou publish a post
Postiz + bridgeprepares each platform
AutoBluesky, Threads: usually posted within a minute
You finishInstagram, TikTok: a draft plus a phone notification with everything ready

There are three tools involved. You only ever need to open the first one to start a post:

  • Ghost (example.com/ghost): where you write posts.
  • Postiz (postiz.example.com): the social media calendar. It holds the drafts and the posts that went out.
  • ntfy: the phone app that sends you the "finish this post" notifications. Subscribe to the site's topic on its ntfy server: whoever set up the site has both (a long random topic, so nobody else can read it).

Quick start

  1. Write the post in Ghost. Write it the way you want it to appear on social, hashtags at the end. Add a feature image, and an audio or video card if you want sound.
  2. Add tags if you need them. For example #bigdrop for a major release, or #nosocial to keep a post off social media. See Tags.
  3. Publish. Set Post access to Public, then publish. Bluesky and Threads are posted automatically.
  4. Finish Instagram and TikTok from your phone. You'll get one notification for each. Save the attached image or video, tap Copy caption, and post it in the app with your music and @tags.

Writing a post in Ghost

Your post text

The social posts use the text you write in the post, as you wrote it: paragraphs, line breaks, emojis and hashtags. A few things to know:

  • No titles. None of these platforms show titles, so the post title isn't included.
  • Formatting: bulleted lists keep their • bullets, numbered lists keep their numbers, and quotes get “quotation marks”. Bold, italics and headings come out as plain text: none of these platforms has text styling.
  • Links in your text: on Bluesky and Threads, where links can be tapped, the address goes after the link text, e.g. "Out now on SoundCloud (https://soundcloud.com/…)". Links to example.com get tagged like the post link. On Instagram and TikTok only the link text shows. The link that becomes the 🎧 Listen line isn't repeated in your text. If adding the addresses would push the post over Bluesky's or Threads' limit (dropping hashtags or shortening your text), they're left out and only the link text shows. The Social preview shows which way each post went.
  • Hashtags go last. Put them in their own paragraph at the end of the post, and they stay at the end of the social posts too, after the links.
  • Cards are left out: images, video, audio, the SoundCloud player and its caption, and bookmark cards. Their links still count (see below).
  • Long posts get fitted. Bluesky allows about 300 characters and Threads 500. If a post is too long, hashtags are dropped first, then your text is shortened with "…". Instagram and TikTok have plenty of room.
  • Want a shorter social text? Write it in the post's Excerpt field (post settings) and add the #useexcerpt tag. The social posts then use the Excerpt instead of your post text. The hashtags from the end of your post are still added. Without the tag, the Excerpt is only used when the post has no text of its own, for example a post that's just a video.

Feature image

The feature image goes out with the post on every platform. JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF all work: the bridge turns them into whatever each app accepts, and scales big photos down. Photos Postiz can't take as they are (AVIF, BMP, TIFF, iPhone HEIC) are turned into JPEGs first. Square or 4:5 portrait images work best on Instagram. When you post it yourself, the app lets you crop. With #autopost, very wide banners get rejected by Instagram.

Several photos: a gallery

Add a gallery card to share several photos. They go out after the feature image, in the gallery's order, up to 10 photos in all (a photo that's both the feature image and in the gallery counts once):

  • Bluesky: the first 4, as a grid (Bluesky's limit).
  • Threads, Instagram: a carousel you swipe through.
  • TikTok: a photo post.

Only gallery cards count: single image cards in the text stay on the website. A post with a video or audio card shares that instead of the photos. For Instagram and TikTok, the notification has the first photo attached and an All N photos button: it opens a page with every photo in order, already cropped the way the app needs it, and Share all hands them all to the app in one go (see Finishing Instagram and TikTok posts). Use Social preview to check what each channel gets.

Adding sound: audio or video

If you want the post to have sound, add one of these to the post body. The post then goes out as a video instead of an image.

  • Audio card (type /audio): upload an MP3, M4A or WAV. Instagram and TikTok can't post an audio file on its own, so the system makes a short vertical video from it: your feature image with a moving waveform, playing your audio. That video is what gets posted, and it's what's attached to your notifications.
    • Audio of 90 seconds or less is used in full, so uploading a short clip is the easiest way.
    • For longer audio, a 30-second clip is used, starting 1 minute in. Use the #clip 12:30 tag to choose a different part.
  • Video card (type /video): upload an MP4. It's shared with its own sound. Ghost refuses MKV (common for OBS recordings) and MOV (iPhone). It does accept WebM, but only MP4s get shared. So convert anything that isn't MP4 first. See below.

Converting a video to MP4

Use whichever of these is easiest:

  • HandBrake (free, for Mac and Windows, at handbrake.fr):
    1. Open HandBrake and drag your video in.
    2. Under Preset, pick General → Fast 1080p30.
    3. Check that Format says MP4, then click Start.
  • On your phone: open the video in CapCut or InShot and export it. They save as MP4. This is also the easiest way to trim a long recording into a short clip.
  • OBS recordings: in OBS, choose File → Remux Recordings, pick the MKV and click Remux. You get an MP4 in seconds, with no quality loss.
Keep uploads small

Uploads to Ghost over 100 MB fail. Don't upload a whole 2-hour set; upload a short clip and link the full set on SoundCloud.

Linking your set: YouTube, SoundCloud or Mixcloud

If the post contains a link to YouTube, SoundCloud or Mixcloud, the social posts mention it. On Bluesky and Threads you get a 🎧 Listen: line with the tappable link. On Instagram and TikTok, where links can't be tapped, you get "🎧 Full set on YouTube + the full post: link in bio". The link can be anywhere in the post: a YouTube video embedded in the post, a caption under the SoundCloud player, a bookmark card, or normal text. YouTube comes first: if the post links to YouTube and SoundCloud, the YouTube link is used, then SoundCloud, then Mixcloud. With several links to the same site, the first is used. To use a different link (Bandcamp, a second YouTube video, anything) or none at all, pick it on the Social preview.

Links carry a tag. The link to your post is a short link with a letter for the network: example.com/b/<the post's slug> on Bluesky, /t/ on Threads, /i/ for the Instagram link sticker, /k/ on TikTok. It takes people straight to the post and tells the site's stats which network each visitor and new member came from (apps often hide it). Leave it on when you edit a caption. For the same reason, use the short links in your bios: example.com/ig, /bsky, /th, /tk, /dis for Discord and /yt for YouTube (they're tagged as bio links). The bridge doesn't post to Discord or YouTube, so when you share a post there yourself, use example.com/d/<the post's slug> on Discord or example.com/y/<the post's slug> on YouTube (in a video's description, a pinned comment or a community post; or /d, /y alone for the home page) and it's counted as a Discord or YouTube post. To group posts into a campaign, add an internal tag like #campaign-summer-launch (see below); its links are then longer.

A caption under the SoundCloud player works well. The link can be the URL itself, or words you've linked:

  • Pasting the URL: Ghost normally turns a pasted SoundCloud URL into a link automatically. You'll see it change colour or get underlined.
  • Linking your own words: for example "grab the full set on SoundCloud". Select the words, press Cmd/Ctrl + K and paste the URL.

The only thing that doesn't work is a URL that stays as plain text, with no link colour. If that happens, select it and press Cmd/Ctrl + K to make it a link.

Tip: newsletters

The SoundCloud player doesn't show up in the email newsletter. Add a linked caption under it, like "Recorded live at the Cochrane session: grab the full set on SoundCloud". That makes the set reachable from the newsletter too, and the social posts pick up the same link.

Who can see the post

Only posts with Post access: Public are shared. Members-only and paid posts are skipped, so paywalled content isn't advertised.

Checking the social posts before you publish

In the editor, click Preview, then the Social preview button in the bottom corner of the page. (If nothing opens, open the preview in its own tab first, then click the button.) It only works in a browser where you're signed in to Ghost Admin; anyone else you send the preview link to sees the draft but not its social preview, and can't change it. A new tab shows each channel's post as it would go out if you published now:

  • the caption on Bluesky, Threads, Instagram and TikTok, with the image, video or audio clip it would use;
  • the Instagram Story too, for posts tagged #story, or #bigdrop (the link-sticker Story you add by hand, with the sticker drawn where it goes);
  • which posts go out by themselves, and which come to you to finish in the app;
  • the character count, and anything that stops a channel (for example "Instagram needs a video or image");
  • the notifications you'd get on your phone.

Each mock-up is laid out like the app (icons included, though they're only pictures), so you can see how the text, links, hashtags and media will sit. Play any video, step through a carousel with the ‹ › arrows (or swipe or scroll), and click a photo to open it full size.

Lining up photos for Instagram: Instagram shows every photo of a post in the first photo's shape (between square-ish 4:5 and wide 1.91:1), so the others get cropped. On the Instagram card, drag a photo to choose which part shows; double-click puts it back in the middle. It's saved straight away, and when you publish, the photos are cropped exactly like that for Instagram (the Postiz draft and the photo in your notification too). The other platforms get the whole photos.

Choosing the Listen link: the 🎧 Listen link menu above the cards shows which link the posts will use. Automatic is the one found in the post (YouTube first, see Linking your set). You can pick any other link in the post, None for no Listen line, or Another address… and type any web address (press Enter or click away to save). The page reloads with every card, and the #bigdrop link sticker, using it. Pick Automatic to go back.

Different text for one channel: tick Modify text for Bluesky (or Threads, Instagram, TikTok) on that channel's card. A box opens with your post text; change it there. It saves as you type and the mock-up updates. When you publish, that channel gets your own text instead of the post text. The post link (or on Instagram and TikTok, the "link in bio" line) is still added after it, unless your text already has it. Untick it to go back to the post text. Until you actually change the text, nothing is saved, so that channel keeps following the post: later edits in Ghost still reach it. It's handy for Bluesky's 300 characters.

Nothing is posted. It shows the post as last saved, so after changing something, wait for Ghost to say Saved and refresh the tab. A post with an audio card takes a few seconds, while the clip is made.

Tags

These are internal tags: they start with #, and readers never see them. Add them in the post settings under Tags. Type the tag and press Enter. Capital letters don't matter.

TagWhat it does
#nosocialKeeps the post off social media completely.
#bigdropFor major releases. A notification of its own reminds you to post an Instagram Story with a link sticker, with the photo or video for it and the link to use.
#storyMakes the Instagram version a Story instead of a feed post.
#autopostPosts to Instagram and TikTok automatically too, with no drafts or notifications. Use it when you don't need to add music or tags. TikTok needs a video or audio card for this; an image-only TikTok post needs extra setup and will otherwise fail.
#useexcerptUses the post's Excerpt as the social text instead of your post text, for a short hook while the full write-up stays on the site. Hashtags from the end of your post are still added.
#campaign-summer-launchGroups the post under a campaign (here "summer-launch") in the Growth dashboard's Campaigns table. Its links on Bluesky, Threads and the Instagram link sticker become the longer form ending in &utm_campaign=summer-launch. Use the same name on every post of a campaign (letters, digits and dashes). Bio links are not affected.
#manualpostThe opposite of #autopost. You only need it if the default has been changed to automatic.
#clip 12:30
#clip 12:30-13:15		Chooses the part of a long audio card to use: a start time (30 seconds from there), or an exact range of up to 90 seconds.

Big-drop reminder

Every #bigdrop post gets its own "🎧 Big drop: add the link Story" notification (as long as Instagram is connected in Postiz), sent first, before the Instagram and TikTok ones, whether Instagram was posted by hand or automatically. It has the Story's photo attached (the first one, uncropped so it fills the Story), or the video, and the buttons Copy sticker link, Open Instagram and Open SoundCloud (or Open post when there's no listen link). See Big drop: the link Story. The one exception is #story + #bigdrop, covered by the Instagram notification above. Story notifications always come in first (the bridge waits a couple of seconds before sending the rest, so they reach your phone in that order): with #story, Instagram's arrives before TikTok's.

📈 Taking off

"📈 Your YouTube video is taking off" (or SoundCloud track, Instagram post and so on) means an older post suddenly picked up: in the last 4 hours or so, or the last day, it gained at least 5% of what it had, and at least 3 times what it usually gets. It says how much it gained and what it usually gets, with buttons to its Post dashboard in Grafana and to the post itself. Nothing to do: it's a good moment to reshare it, pin it or post something like it. You get one per take-off; the Post dashboard shows how it goes.

📊 Your week

Every Monday at 9 in the morning (the site's time zone) you get "📊 Your week on example.com" with how the week before (Monday to Sunday) went, so you don't have to open Grafana to know:

  • 🌐 the site's visitors (and how that compares with the week before) and new members,
  • 👥 followers gained or lost on each network,
  • 📣 how many posts went out, and the best three by vs your usual,
  • 📖 the three most-read pages,
  • 🎧 your live shows, if you had any: how many, how long, and the most listeners at once.

The Open Grafana button takes you to the Overview dashboard for more (the Analytics Guide explains it). Nothing to do: it's only a summary. A line is left out when its numbers couldn't be read that week. To get it on another day or time, or not at all, ask whoever looks after the server (it's one setting).

⚠️ Warnings

"⚠️ Postiz needs attention" means a post couldn't go out: a platform rejected it, or an account needs reconnecting. "⚠️ Postiz: … failed on …" means the post couldn't even be handed to Postiz. Tap either to open Postiz, then see Something went wrong?

Finishing Instagram and TikTok posts

  1. Save the attachment. Tap the notification to open it in ntfy. Tap ⋯ (top right of the message), then Save file. That puts it in your phone's Downloads. ntfy's own Download button only keeps it inside ntfy, and tapping the preview just zooms in.
    Several photos? Tap Copy caption first, then All N photos. On the page that opens, tap Share all N photos and pick Instagram (Feed) or TikTok: the app opens with all the photos, in order, so skip the next two steps' picking and go on to music and @tags. Rather keep them on the phone first? Tap Save photo 1 of N once per photo: each tap puts the next one in Downloads, in order (browsers only allow one download per tap), and a ✓ marks the ones saved. The page works for 14 days.
  2. Tap "Copy caption". The whole caption is now on your clipboard.
  3. Create the post in the app. On Instagram: + → Post (or Reel, or Story). At the top of the photo grid, tap Recents, switch to Download, and pick the file. On TikTok: + → Upload, then look in the Download folder the same way.
  4. Add music and @tags. If the attachment is a video (from a video card, or the one made from your audio card), it already has sound, so skip the music. Image posts need music, which you add from the app's music picker. Tag the artists using the app's @ search.
  5. Paste the caption and share.
  6. Leave the draft in Postiz. It's tagged Post in app, so the calendar shows it went out by hand, and it stays as your record. Just don't click Post on it, or it would go out a second time. If you decided the post doesn't need music or tags, you can post the draft from Postiz instead of using the app.

Big drop: the link Story

Instagram Stories are the only place on Instagram where a link can be tapped. On #bigdrop posts:

  1. Open the "🎧 Big drop: add the link Story" notification and save its attachment (⋯ → Save file). It's the first photo uncropped, so it fills the Story, or the video.
  2. Instagram: + → Story, then pick it from the Download album.
  3. Tap the sticker icon, then Link. Paste the link (tap Copy sticker link in the notification).
  4. Tap Customize sticker text and write something like "🎧 Listen now", then share.

Using Postiz

Log in at postiz.example.com. The calendar shows every post: the ones that went out, the ones that failed, and drafts. If a day looks empty, expand it, because several posts can stack up.

  • Drafts never publish on their own. Open one to edit it or post it. Instagram and TikTok drafts carry the Post in app tag: those are the ones you finish on your phone.
  • The "Post in app" tag has to exist once. If it's missing, create it: open any post in Postiz, add a tag named exactly Post in app and pick a colour. Until it exists, drafts just arrive without it.
  • Resending a failed post: open it and click Post now. If that isn't offered, use Duplicate Post from the preview and post the copy.
  • Reconnecting an account (for example after Instagram logs you out): click Add Channel and choose the same platform again. Postiz updates the existing channel, and your posts stay attached to it. Don't delete the channel.
    • For Instagram and Threads, log into both in your browser first, as the account you post as, then connect. It saves a lot of login headaches.
  • Posting something new directly from Postiz also works. Only posts that start in Ghost go through the automatic flow.

Good habits

  • Pick music that represents you. A track from the set, or one clearly in your style. Avoid random trending songs.
  • Use an Instagram Creator account, not Business. Creator accounts get the full music library.
  • Keep your bio links current. On Instagram, add example.com and your SoundCloud profile under Edit profile → Links. The site's home page always shows the newest post, so "link in bio" keeps working. On TikTok, bio links need 1,000 followers or a Business account.
  • Mind the copyright. Clips of mixes contain other artists' tracks, so Instagram and TikTok sometimes mute them. Short clips and your own edits get through more often. Music picked from the apps' own libraries is always allowed.
  • Engage. Replying to comments and resharing when artists tag you does more for growth than posting more often.

Something went wrong?

I published, but nothing went out anywhere.
Check that the post is Public and doesn't have #nosocial. Posts that were already shared once won't go out again. If none of that is it, ask whoever maintains the system to check the bridge logs.
Bluesky or Threads says posted, but I can't see it.
Give it a minute, then check your profile, not the home feed. If you got a ⚠️ notification, the post failed, so resend it from Postiz. If there's no warning and it still isn't there after 10 minutes, tell the maintainer: the part of Postiz that sends posts may need a restart.
I didn't get the Instagram or TikTok notification.
Check that you're subscribed to the site's topic in the ntfy app. Also check that Android allows ntfy notifications, isn't battery-restricting it, and that the topic isn't muted. The account also needs to be connected in Postiz, otherwise no notification is sent for it.
The notification doesn't mention a Postiz draft, or the draft isn't in the calendar.
Postiz couldn't save the draft, usually because that account needs reconnecting (see Using Postiz). The notification itself still has everything you need to post by hand.
My video wasn't used; the post went out with the image.
Only MP4 videos are used. Convert other formats to MP4 and upload them again in a video card.
I want to share an old post again.
Post it from Postiz, or ask the maintainer to clear it from the "already shared" list so that republishing in Ghost shares it again.

Glossary

Ghost
The website and newsletter system behind example.com.
Postiz
The social media scheduler. It connects to each platform and holds drafts and the post history.
The bridge
A small helper that notices new Ghost posts, prepares each platform's version and sends the notifications.
ntfy
The notification app on your phone (ntfy.sh, or the site's own ntfy server).
Draft
A post saved in Postiz that won't publish until you tell it to.
Internal tag
A Ghost tag starting with #. Readers never see it; it tells the system what to do.
Audio card / Video card
Blocks in the Ghost editor for uploading sound or video into a post.
Link sticker
A tappable link you add to an Instagram Story. It can only be added in the Instagram app.
