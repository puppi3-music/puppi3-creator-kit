The kit's theme

The kit's theme is Source, the theme Ghost itself makes and ships, with a few things added so the site works with the rest of the kit. Anything Ghost's own help says about Source is true here too. The kit adds:

  • the LIVE NOW bar, which shows while you're streaming and plays the stream on the page;
  • the Social preview button on a draft's preview, which shows what publishing would post to each network;
  • icons beside the links to your other platforms in the footer;
  • counting: which links visitors follow off the site, and plays and hiccups on the LIVE NOW bar. These go to the dashboards (the Analytics guide says where), with nothing about who the visitor is.
  • a credit line in the footer, under "Powered by Ghost": Theme: Puppi3's Creator Kit by DJ Puppi3. The kit is free to use and change, and its licence asks for one thing in return: keep that line.
  • a Source link beside it, to the kit's code on GitHub. The licence (the AGPL) asks a site to offer its visitors the code it runs: this link does that for you. If you change the kit, point it at your changed copy.

Installing it

Whoever set up the site does this once (the Setup guide has it): download puppi3-creator-kit-theme.zip from the kit's release for your version (kit/VERSION; a theme you've changed: kit/theme-zip.sh builds it in kit/dist/), and you upload it in Ghost Admin under Settings → Theme → Change theme → Upload theme, then Activate.

When the kit is updated

A new version of the kit may bring a new theme: download its zip from the new version's release and upload it the same way (the Operations guide says how to tell). Ghost asks whether to overwrite the theme it has: Overwrite. It keeps your settings, since it's the same theme. Changes made to the theme's files yourself are lost then: see Another theme for the way to keep them.

Its settings

Everything here is in Ghost Admin, needs no code and is live as soon as you save. Most of it is under Settings → Design & branding → Customize, which shows the site beside the settings as you change them. Customize has two tabs: Brand, Ghost's own, and Theme, the theme's settings, in three parts: Site wide, Homepage and Post.

Brand

Brand, the first tab: the accent colour, the icon (the small picture in the browser's tab), the logo, the cover image, and Typography: a heading font and a body font from Ghost's own list. The accent colour is used for buttons and links all over the site, and for the kit's parts too: the LIVE NOW bar, the Social preview button and the platform links follow it. The site's name and description aren't here: they're in Settings → Title & description.

Elsewhere in Settings

  • Settings → Title & description, Timezone and Social accounts (under General settings): the site's name and description, its time zone, and your accounts on the big networks, which show as icons in the footer.
  • Settings → Navigation: the menu at the top (Primary) and the links in the footer (Secondary), where the links to your other platforms go.
  • Settings → Access and Signup portal (under Membership): who can sign up, and the sign-up window with its button. Settings → Newsletters: how the newsletter looks.

The LIVE NOW bar

While you're streaming, a LIVE NOW bar appears at the top of every page, with a play button, a volume slider (phones use their own volume buttons) and the track playing, when your streaming software sends it. It shows within 15 seconds of you going on air, on pages already open too, and goes away when you stop.

Switching it on

It needs the site's live module (whoever set up the site switches it on). Then set Live stream URL (Customize's Theme tab, Site wide) to the stream's address: https://live.example.com/stream-mp3, with your own site's address for example.com. Empty the setting to switch the bar off; the stream itself keeps working.

What listeners hear

When someone presses play, the bar waits a few seconds before the sound starts, so short hiccups in their connection aren't heard later. If their connection drops, it picks up again by itself while you're still on air. Each play and each hiccup is counted on the dashboards (How did my live show go?).

The bar doesn't show

  • Is the Live stream URL set? Without it, there's no bar at all.
  • Is your streaming software connected? The bar shows only while it's sending. The stream server's own page, at https://live.example.com, lists what's connected.
  • Pages already open look again every 15 seconds; reloading the page looks at once.

Social preview

With the social module, a draft's preview has a Social preview button in the bottom corner, in your accent colour. In the editor, click Preview, then the button: a new tab shows each network's post as it would go out if you published now, and lets you change it. The Posting guide has the rest.

It works only where you're signed in to Ghost Admin. Anyone you send the preview link to sees the draft, and the button, but not the social preview.

Links to your other platforms

Ghost's Social accounts (Settings → Social accounts, under General settings) cover the big networks (Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, Threads and so on), not the other places a creator is: a membership page, a shop, a newsletter, a portfolio, a podcast, a music profile. For those, add them to the footer's links: Settings → Navigation → Secondary, a label (e.g. "Patreon") and your page's full address. Links to these get their icon in front:

Video and streams: Vimeo, Twitch. Support and newsletters: Patreon, Ko-fi, Substack. Shops and portfolios: Etsy, Behance. Community: Discord. Podcasts and music: Apple Podcasts, Spotify, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, Bandcamp (your name.bandcamp.com too), Beatport, Resident Advisor (ra.co).

Links to anything else show as plain text links, as they would anyway. Clicks on them, and on any link to another site, are counted: Do people click through to my work elsewhere?

Short links

The kit comes with short links on your own site that tell the dashboards where a visitor came from. Apps like Instagram and TikTok often hide that; a link that names the network itself doesn't lose it. Whoever set up the site uploads them once (the file kit/redirects.yaml, in Settings → Labs (under Advanced) → Open → Beta features → Redirects → Upload redirects file).

Bio links

Put these in each network's profile, where it asks for your website:

Each one opens your home page, and the visit counts as coming from that network's bio.

Links in posts

When the kit shares a post, links to your site in it go out as short links too: example.com/b/your-post on Bluesky, /t/ on Threads, /i/ on Instagram, /k/ on TikTok. Nothing to do: they open the post, and the visit counts as coming from that network's posts. Sharing a post by hand on Discord or YouTube, put /d/ or /y/ in front of its path the same way (example.com/d/your-post).

The dashboards show bio and post visits apart: Posts vs bio links. To share plain links instead, whoever runs the site sets TAG_LINKS to false.

Redirects of your own

Ghost takes a single redirects file, and uploading one replaces the one before. To add your own (an old address that moved, say), click Edit beside Upload redirects file: it opens the current file, with the kit's short links in it; add your lines and Save. When an update of the kit changes its short links, add its new lines the same way rather than uploading the kit's file over yours.

Using another theme

Any Ghost theme works on a kit site: upload it in place of the kit's. The site, its members and newsletters, the visitor numbers, sharing to the networks, the notifications and the short links don't depend on the theme. What a theme without the kit's parts doesn't have:

PartWithout it
LIVE NOW barNo bar; the stream still plays at its own address, for anyone who has it
Social preview buttonThe social preview is still there: on a draft's preview page, change /p/ in the address to /ghost/social-preview/
Link and play countingThe dashboards' links followed off the site, and the bar's plays and hiccups, stay empty
Platform link iconsThe links show as the theme shows them

Adding the kit's parts to it

This part is for whoever edits the theme's files. The kit's parts are files of their own, so they move into another theme in a few steps. kit/theme/KIT.md lists them, and the edits to Source's files, exactly:

  1. Copy assets/kit/ and partials/kit/ from kit/theme/ into your theme.
  2. In its default.hbs: kit/kit.css after the theme's own styles; {{> "kit/icons"}} and {{> "kit/live-bar"}} at the top of <body>; kit/kit.js and then, only when the Live stream URL is set, kit/live-bar.js at the end. The kit's default.hbs shows each, marked with a "Puppi3's Creator Kit" comment.
  3. In its package.json: the live_stream_url setting under config.custom, as in the kit's. Ghost allows a theme 20 settings. If yours has no room, write the stream's address into partials/kit/live-bar.hbs in place of {{@custom.live_stream_url}}, and take out the {{#if @custom.live_stream_url}} around the bar there and around kit/live-bar.js in default.hbs: without the setting they'd leave the bar off.
  4. The footer's platform link icons need the theme's footer menu to have the class gh-footer-menu (Source's), or that name changed in kit.js. Everything else works with any theme. The kit's styles use Source's --ghost-accent-color, which Ghost sets on every theme, and its gh-outer/gh-inner page widths, which other themes may lack: kit.css is short, and the bar's layout is at its top.

Check it the way the kit's own theme is checked: gscan (Ghost's theme checker) on the zip, then a look at a draft's preview and a stream.

Changing the kit's theme itself

To change the kit's theme beyond its settings, make it your own: copy kit/theme/ to a folder of yours, give it a new name in its package.json, and zip and upload that. Keep the footer's credit line in it: the kit's licence (the LICENSE file at the top of the kit; the theme's own LICENSE is Source's) asks changed versions to keep it, and, if your site runs a changed version, to offer its visitors the changed code. Updates of the kit then don't overwrite it; bring over what an update changes in kit/theme/ when you want it.
