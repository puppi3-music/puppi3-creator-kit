How it fits together

When you publish a post in Ghost, Ghost tells the bridge, and the bridge hands the post to Postiz for each network connected there: as the kit comes, Bluesky and Threads are posted, and Instagram and TikTok become drafts with a phone notification (the Posting guide has the rest). Postiz keeps the networks' sign-ins. For each network you make an app of your own on its developer site, put the app's keys in secrets/postiz.env, and then connect the account in Postiz.

The numbers on the dashboards come from the same sign-ins where they can. For Instagram, Threads, TikTok and YouTube, the bridge reads the channel's sign-in from Postiz's database (with POSTIZ_DB_PASSWORD, which setup made). Bluesky and Mixcloud give their numbers to anyone. SoundCloud needs an app of the bridge's own, and your site, Cloudflare and Google Search a key each.

Which networks the dashboards expect is NETWORKS in site.env: one listed there but not set up shows as an alert error until you set it up or take it out. Cloudflare and Google Search aren't in that list; their numbers appear once their keys are there. Every key is in the Settings reference.

After changing secrets/postiz.env

Start the site again: kit/kit.sh <folder> up -d, or k8s/up.sh <folder> <overlay>. Postiz and the bridge read the file only when they start. A channel connected or reconnected in Postiz needs nothing more: the bridge finds it at its next check (hourly; the first a minute or two after it starts).

The networks' developer sites move their menus around now and then. The names below were right in 2026; what matters is that every permission, address and key ends up where each part says.

Postiz

Your Postiz account, its API key and closing its sign-ups are in the Setup guide. Then, for every network:

  • Connecting a channel: in Postiz, Add Channel, pick the network, sign in as the account and allow everything it asks for. The network's app (below; Bluesky needs none) must be there first, with its keys in secrets/postiz.env and the site started again, or the network's sign-in page fails at once.
  • The redirect address of every app is https://postiz.<your site>/integrations/social/<name>, where the name is instagram-standalone, threads, tiktok or youtube: https, and no slash at the end. Postiz makes it from POSTIZ_DOMAIN (the bare name, postiz.<your site>) and has no setting for it, so it goes in the app exactly like that, in the box for the sign-in redirect. Each network's part says which box; a look-alike "website" box doesn't count.
  • A sign-in that fails with a redirect error: the address of the network's error page holds what Postiz sent, after redirect_uri= (written with %3A%2F%2F for :// and %2F for /). Compare it with the app's.
  • Reconnecting (a sign-in that ran out, a permission added later): Add Channel again with the same account. Postiz updates the channel it has, so its posts stay with it. The channel menu's Reconnect channel only shows when Postiz itself has flagged the sign-in.
  • Sign in to the network in the same browser first. It saves a lot of trouble, and on a phone a network's own app can take over the sign-in link: connect from a computer.

Instagram

Posts and numbers, through an app on Meta's developer site and the Instagram (Standalone) channel in Postiz.

On Meta's side

  1. The account must be a professional one: Creator or Business (in the Instagram app's settings).
  2. developers.facebook.com/apps → Create app, with the use case Manage messaging & content on Instagram (API setup with Instagram login).
  3. Permissions (the use case's Permissions and features): instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish, instagram_business_manage_comments and instagram_business_manage_insights. Add all four: the last one is the numbers.
  4. The redirect address goes under Business login settings (step 3 or 4 of the setup page): https://postiz.<your site>/integrations/social/instagram-standalone. The webhook "verify token" boxes aren't needed.
  5. App roles → Roles → Instagram Tester: add the account, then accept the invite at instagram.com/accounts/manage_access/ → Tester invites.

Leave the app in development mode: it works for your own accounts with no App Review, and only accounts with a tester role can connect to it.

The keys, and Postiz

Use the Instagram app ID and secret shown on the API setup with Instagram login page, not the main App ID at the top of the app: INSTAGRAM_APP_ID and INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET, then start the site again. With the main App ID, Instagram's sign-in says the app or its redirect is invalid. Then, in Postiz: Add Channel → Instagram (Standalone), sign in as the account and allow everything, insights included.

The numbers

Nothing more to set up: followers, reach, profile views and link taps per day, every post and Story, and the audience (country, age, gender, when followers are online). Instagram leaves out new followers per day while an account has under 100 followers.

When it goes wrong

  • "The account is missing some permissions…" in Postiz, or a permission error in Instagram's status on the Platforms dashboard: the account is locked or restricted by Meta, or its sign-in is stale. Once it's unlocked, Add Channel → Instagram (Standalone) again and allow everything. Failed posts aren't tried again by themselves: send them again.
  • The tester invite "can't find" the account: it isn't a professional account, or the name has a typo. Switch it to Creator, and type the exact username without the @.

What this connection can't do: add Instagram's licensed music or a link sticker (those are added in the app: the Posting guide says how). Photos go to Instagram as JPEG, at most 1440 wide and 8 MB, between 4:5 and 1.91:1: the bridge converts, scales and crops them to fit. Story videos are up to 60 seconds.

Threads

Posts and numbers, through a second Meta app.

On Meta's side

  1. A separate Meta app, with the use case Access the Threads API.
  2. Permissions: threads_basic, threads_content_publish, threads_manage_replies and threads_manage_insights. Add all four: the last one is the numbers.
  3. Settings: the redirect, uninstall and delete callback addresses all https://postiz.<your site>/integrations/social/threads. Press Enter after each, so it becomes a tag.
  4. Roles → Threads Tester: add the account, then accept it in Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions → Invites.

The keys, and Postiz

The Threads app ID and secret (App settings → Basic, or Customize → Settings), not the main App ID: THREADS_APP_ID and THREADS_APP_SECRET, then start the site again. Before connecting, sign in to instagram.com and threads.com in the browser as the account: on a phone the Threads app takes over the sign-in link, and on a computer Threads may show only a QR code. Then Add Channel → Threads.

The numbers

Nothing more to set up: views, likes, replies, reposts and quotes per day, followers, link clicks and every post's numbers.

When it goes wrong

  • "The link's not working or the page is gone" when connecting: the main App ID instead of the Threads one, the tester invite not accepted, a permission missing, or not signed in to Threads in the browser.
  • A permission error in Threads' status on the Platforms dashboard: threads_manage_insights is missing, or was added after connecting. Add it, then connect again.

TikTok

Posts (as drafts in your TikTok inbox) and numbers, through an app on developers.tiktok.com. Sign in there with any TikTok account; the app can then be used with yours.

The app

  1. Manage apps → Connect an app (a personal app is fine). Fill in its details: a name, icon, category and description, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy addresses (pages on your site will do), and platform Web. Under Configure for Web/Desktop, the Web/Desktop URL is your site's address, https://<your site>: that box is the app's website, not its redirect address.
  2. Switch to Sandbox first, with the Production / Sandbox switch at the top of the app's page (make a sandbox if there's none, any name). Sandbox and Production each have their own products, permissions, redirect address and keys, so everything below goes in the sandbox. A sandbox works for the accounts you add to it, with every permission, without TikTok reviewing anything. Don't submit the app for review: that wants demo videos, and only adds public posting straight to the profile, which the kit doesn't use. A page asking for a video of the whole flow means you're on Production.
  3. Target users (in the sandbox): add your TikTok account, then accept the invite in the TikTok app.
  4. Add products (on the Sandbox tab): Login Kit and Content Posting API. Then:
    • Login Kit → Redirect URI: https://postiz.<your site>/integrations/social/tiktok, then Add a URI. The box is in Login Kit's own settings, and only shows once Login Kit is added and Web is ticked under Platforms.
    • Content Posting API → Direct Post: turn it on. Postiz asks for video.publish, which only appears then, and the sign-in fails without it.
  5. Permissions (scopes): all six that Postiz asks for, which come from different places:
    ScopeForWhere you find it
    user.info.basicConnecting: your name and pictureComes with Login Kit, often greyed out in the list; nothing to do
    user.info.profileNumbers: the profile link and nameAdd scopes, under Login Kit
    user.info.statsNumbers: followers, likes, videosAdd scopes, under Login Kit
    video.listNumbers: every video's views, likes, comments and sharesAdd scopes, under Login Kit
    video.uploadPosting: drafts in your TikTok inboxComes with Content Posting API
    video.publishPostiz asks for it, so the sign-in fails without it (the kit sends drafts)Once Direct Post is on, then add it
    If one won't appear, check you're on the Sandbox tab and both products are added.
  6. Save, with the button at the top of the page: nothing counts until it's saved.
  7. The sandbox's Client key and Client secret (shown while Sandbox is selected; Production's are different) go in TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID and TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET; then start the site again.
  8. In Postiz: Add Channel → TikTok, sign in as the account and allow every permission. A channel connected before the numbers' permissions were added needs Add Channel → TikTok again: TikTok only grants new permissions at a new sign-in.

Posting

An app TikTok hasn't reviewed can only post privately, so posts that go out by themselves land in your TikTok inbox as drafts, for you to publish in the app. Photo posts that go out by themselves (#autopost on a post with no video or audio) have TikTok fetch the photos from Postiz, which needs https://postiz.<your site>/ verified as a URL prefix in TikTok's developer portal.

The numbers

Followers, following, likes and videos come from Postiz's own numbers for the channel, which also renews TikTok's sign-in (it lasts 23 hours); then every video's views, likes, comments and shares. TikTok gives nothing per day, so followers and likes per day start the day the bridge began collecting.

When it goes wrong

  • "We couldn't log in with TikTok … correct the following: redirect_uri": most often the address went in the Web/Desktop URL box instead of Login Kit's Redirect URI. Otherwise it isn't on the tab whose keys you used: the error page's address has client_key=, which says whose keys Postiz has (the sandbox's), and redirect_uri=, which must be exactly what that tab's Login Kit has.
  • A scope error from Postiz's sign-in: the one it names is still missing in the sandbox.
  • In TikTok's status on the Platforms dashboard: no TikTok channel in Postiz (not connected, or switched off there); needs reconnecting or access_token_invalid (Add Channel → TikTok again); scope_not_authorized (a numbers permission is missing or came after connecting: add it, connect again); Postiz analytics: no numbers (usually a sign-in that's gone: connect again).
  • Account numbers but no videos: the bridge can't read Postiz's database. kit/kit.sh <folder> exec -T postiz-ghost-bridge printenv POSTIZ_DB_PASSWORD | wc -c should be more than 1.

Bluesky

Posts and numbers, with no app to make.

  • In Bluesky: Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords → add one (call it "Postiz"), and copy it: it's shown once.
  • In Postiz: Add Channel → Bluesky, with your handle and the app password (never your own password). Nothing goes in secrets/postiz.env.
  • The numbers come from Bluesky's public numbers, for the Bluesky channel in Postiz. For another account, or before connecting it, put the handle in BLUESKY_HANDLE (example.com or name.bsky.social) and start the site again.

A post there is at most 300 characters (the bridge shortens the text to fit), and a video up to about 3 minutes and 100 MB. Posting video needs a verified email on the Bluesky account.

YouTube

Numbers only: the bridge doesn't post to YouTube. It needs an app in Google Cloud and the channel connected in Postiz.

On Google's side

  1. console.cloud.google.com: make a project, then APIs & Services → Library: turn on YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API.
  2. The OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform): External, the app's name and your email. Under Data access the scopes can stay empty (Postiz asks for them at sign-in). Under Audience, press Publish app, so it says In production: an app left in Testing loses its sign-ins after 7 days. Google's verification isn't needed for your own channel: at sign-in, Google warns "Google hasn't verified this app"; choose Advanced → Go to … (unsafe).
  3. Clients → Create client: type Web application, with the authorised redirect URI https://postiz.<your site>/integrations/social/youtube (in Authorised redirect URIs, not Authorised JavaScript origins; a mismatch shows as Google's "Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch").
  4. Its client ID and secret go in YOUTUBE_CLIENT_ID and YOUTUBE_CLIENT_SECRET; then start the site again.

In Postiz

Add Channel → YouTube: sign in with the Google account that owns the channel, allow everything (Postiz checks it got every scope), then pick the channel. For a brand account's channel, pick the brand account at Google's account chooser.

The numbers

Nothing more to set up. YouTube fills in each day two or three days late. In YouTube's status on the Platforms dashboard, "needs YOUTUBE_CLIENT_ID and YOUTUBE_CLIENT_SECRET" means they aren't in the file or the site wasn't started again; a sign-in that stopped after a week means the app is still in Testing: publish it, then connect again.

SoundCloud

Numbers only. Postiz has no SoundCloud, so the bridge signs in with an app of its own:

  1. Sign in to SoundCloud as the account (registering an app needs Artist Pro), then soundcloud.com/you/apps → Register a new application. Any name. The redirect address isn't used (the bridge signs in as the app itself), so https://<your site>/ will do.
  2. Its client ID and secret go in SOUNDCLOUD_CLIENT_ID and SOUNDCLOUD_CLIENT_SECRET, and the profile's address in SOUNDCLOUD_USER (https://soundcloud.com/<name>); then start the site again.

Within a minute, SoundCloud's status on the Platforms dashboard shows the first collection, or Networks not shown at its bottom says what went wrong: "invalid_client" is a wrong ID or secret, "is SOUNDCLOUD_USER the profile address?" a profile it couldn't find, and "too many sign-ins" means waiting (it resets within 12 hours).

Mixcloud

Numbers only, from Mixcloud's public numbers: no app and no sign-in. Put the profile's address in MIXCLOUD_USER (https://www.mixcloud.com/<name>/) and start the site again. Mixcloud gives only totals, so the numbers per day start the day the bridge began collecting.

Your site

Numbers only: the dashboards treat the site as one more network, so its posts get the same Post dashboard and When to post as the others. Its posts, members and newsletters come from Ghost with GHOST_ADMIN_API_KEY, the Admin API key of the integration you made in the Setup guide (the Social preview uses the same key). Its visitors come from the analytics module, which the bridge reads by itself: TINYBIRD_TOKEN stays empty.

In its status on the Platforms dashboard, "needs GHOST_API_URL and GHOST_ADMIN_API_KEY" means the key isn't in secrets/postiz.env, and "needs TINYBIRD_TOKEN" that the analytics module is off.

Cloudflare

For the Traffic dashboard's Everything Cloudflare answered, on a site behind Cloudflare. The bridge needs a read-only token of its own (the one Traefik has for certificates can't read the numbers, and shouldn't):

  1. In Cloudflare: My Profile → API Tokens → Create Token → Create Custom Token. Permission Zone → Analytics → Read; zone resources Include → Specific zone → your site. Create it and copy it.
  2. The zone's ID is on your site's Overview page in Cloudflare (right column, under API).
  3. Put them in CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN and CLOUDFLARE_ZONE_ID, and start the site again.

Within a minute or two, Cloudflare's status on the Traffic dashboard shows the first collection (30 days back), or what went wrong.

Google Search

For the Traffic dashboard's Google Search. The bridge signs in as a Google Cloud service account, which you add to your site's Search Console property as a user:

  1. If the site isn't in Search Console yet, add it as a Domain property and verify it with the TXT record it gives you (in your DNS). Google has numbers only from then on.
  2. In Google Cloud (YouTube's project will do): APIs & Services → Library → turn on the Google Search Console API. Then IAM & Admin → Service Accounts → Create service account (no roles), open it, Keys → Add key → JSON: that downloads its key.
  3. In Search Console: the property → Settings → Users and permissions → Add user, with the service account's email (…@….iam.gserviceaccount.com) and the permission Restricted (read-only).
  4. Turn the key into one line, base64 -i key.json | tr -d '\n', and put that in SEARCH_CONSOLE_KEY. SEARCH_CONSOLE_SITE is sc-domain:<your site> for a Domain property (setup filled it in); for a URL-prefix property, it's the property's address. Start the site again, then delete the downloaded key.

Its status on the Traffic dashboard shows the first collection, or what went wrong: a 403 means the service account isn't a user of the property, or the API isn't on.

Checking the numbers

  • Grafana → Platforms. A network's section shows once the bridge has numbers for it. Its status panel says when they were last collected, and what's wrong if anything is.
  • A network with no numbers yet has no section. Networks not shown, at the bottom of the dashboard, lists it with the bridge's reason (like "no TikTok channel in Postiz" or "needs POSTIZ_DB_PASSWORD").
  • Straight from the bridge, for a closer look (or instagram, threads, bluesky, youtube, soundcloud, mixcloud, ghost, cloudflare, search): updatedAt says when, errors what went wrong. The bridge's log has the same errors. 
    kit/kit.sh <folder> exec -T postiz-ghost-bridge wget -qO- http://localhost:8080/tiktok.json | head -c 600
kit/kit.sh <folder> logs postiz-ghost-bridge | grep "stats:"
    On Kubernetes: kubectl -n <short name> exec deploy/postiz-ghost-bridge -- wget -qO- http://localhost:8080/tiktok.json.

Keeping it safe

  • Postiz's API key (POSTIZ_API_KEY) can post to every network you've connected. It's only in secrets/postiz.env and the bridge. To change it: a new one in Postiz (Settings → Developers), then the file, then start the site again.
  • The Ghost key (GHOST_ADMIN_API_KEY) could change the site's content (Ghost has no read-only keys); the bridge only reads with it. A new one (the integration in Ghost → Admin API key → Regenerate) goes in the file too.
  • The webhook's secret: change it in Ghost's integration and in GHOST_WEBHOOK_SECRET together. The bridge refuses every request that isn't signed with it, and every request when it has none.
  • The networks' app secrets: a new one goes in the file, then start the site again; channels may need connecting again.
  • Postiz's own sign-in is on the internet: keep its sign-ups closed (POSTIZ_DISABLE_REGISTRATION=true) and its password strong.
  • Postiz's uploads must stay public: Instagram and Threads fetch each post's pictures and videos from https://postiz.<your site>/uploads/…. Don't put Postiz behind a sign-in or a home-only rule without leaving /uploads out.
  • The photo pages linked from your notifications need no sign-in (your phone may not be signed in to Ghost): a long random address is their key, sent only in your notification, and they show only photos that are public anyway. They stop working after 14 days (PHOTO_PAGE_DAYS). A draft's Social preview is only for staff signed in to Ghost Admin.
  • Temporal's page, which shows Postiz's publishing jobs, has no sign-in, so it only listens on the server itself: http://127.0.0.1:8233 there (from another computer, through ssh: the Operations guide). Keep it that way.
